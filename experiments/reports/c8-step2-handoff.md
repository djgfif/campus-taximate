The actual production Packer uses greedy initialization **and bounded DFS**, with a 100,000-node budget and wall time, in addition to the candidate and secondary-verification limits. The benchmark invokes this unchanged implementation; it does not replace it with a greedy reimplementation.

ROUND-OPT-SAVING and the hindsight secondary objective maximize **expected quote savings**; reported realized-looking metrics are C7 seeded TIMS residual proxies, never observed payments. Expected and residual-adjusted money tables are both included. Hindsight preserves per-rider departure windows (instant windows derive from possible minute confirmation plus preparation/dispatch), but relaxes round confirmation/early-confirm rules. Its matched-rider count is an exact upper bound within this model; its savings and delay are values of the selected solution, not independent bounds. C7 tariff and demand assumptions remain: one male hub pool, fitted shared-route fare versus Pocheon solo tariff, static routing, and proxy population demand.
